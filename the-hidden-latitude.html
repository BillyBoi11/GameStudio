About

You wake up on a remote island system with no explanation of how you arrived. The world is silent, but not empty. Structures change when unobserved, and reality itself appears unstable.

A survivor named Elliot communicates through a radio, guiding you through an interconnected system of islands, towers, and sealed environments. Churches act as focal points of corruption, triggering environmental and psychological disturbances when interacted with.

As exploration continues, you uncover that the island is part of a larger system controlling doors, access points, and hidden areas across multiple landmasses. Not everything guiding you can be trusted.
